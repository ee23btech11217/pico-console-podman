Clock and core voltage: 264 MHz @ 1.15 V

RP2350 datasheet, Fig. 156, DVDD current vs core frequency

• 264 MHz gives SPI divider 4 = 66 MHz
• 1.15 V; ﬂash clock kept ≤ 100 MHz
• Current rises with clock: no extra MHz

CPU cost vs clock, 150-400 MHz (docs/perf/03)

Clock guard: a 3 s watchdog over the ﬁrst 5 s of frames; a hang reboots into

Audio: PWM DAC + stored music

Music footprint, interrupt cost at 264 MHz (docs/perf/15)

• GP15 → 1 kΩ → NPN → 8 Ω speaker
• 31.25 kHz 8-bit PWM DAC interrupt
• 4-bit IMA ADPCM, 15.6 kHz, from ﬂash
• 1.6 % of core 0 while playing
• Tempo follows the sphere level

Decoder self-test at boot: the ﬁrst second of track 1 is decoded on the
board and its checksum compared with the packer's (selftest 1 = bitexact). Loudness measured in the interrupt on the real output (musicrms 40/127), and a DWT cycle counter gives the interrupt's cost (137
cycles).

• Whole tracks: 160 s + 264 s (R12)

SOFTWARE

Music: whole tracks, louder where the speaker plays

docs/perf/26, packs decoded on the PC (tools/music_levels.py)

• Whole tracks: 75 s → 160 s and 264 s
• 3,281 KB of the 3,580 KB music area
• +1.8 / +1.9 dB above 200 Hz, same peaks

DEBUG DETAIL

The tracks already hit the limiter: +7 dB of drive gave only +0.7 dB RMS. The
headroom was in bass the 8 Ω speaker cannot play: a 4-pole high-pass at 180 Hz and
+3 dB at 2 kHz free the limiter for the audible band. On the board: SND ... music-rms.

RP2350 datasheet, Fig. 36, clocks

DATASHEET ERRATA

RP2350-E9 and E12 applied to the sleep
• E9: input pad between VIL/VIH leaks ~120 µA
• Speaker pin: driven low, input oﬀ
• All other pads: input oﬀ, pulls oﬀ
• E12: clk_sys ≥ 1.1 × clk_usb → 64 MHz
• Die temp falls in sleep: 60.2 → 57.1 °C

RP2350 datasheet, Fig. 157, pad leakage (E9, chip A2)

DEBUG DETAIL

The log prints the stepping (chip A2) and how many pads changed (pads IE-oﬀ 9). A static_assert pins the E12 rule. Every datasheet-derived change is
listed in docs/datasheet/IMPLEMENTATION.md.

HARDWARE

Measuring die temperature correctly
• Sensor: ADC ch. 4, 0.706 V @ 27 °C
• −1.721 mV/°C (datasheet §12.4.6)
• GPIO23 high: regulator in PWM mode
• Up to +11 °C error removed

Same run read both ways (docs/perf/22)

DEBUG DETAIL

At low load the Pico 2's 3.3 V regulator switches to power-save mode; its ripple moved the ADC reference. Holding GPIO23 high for 2 ms around each
read gave steady values (60.5-62.7 °C asleep).
